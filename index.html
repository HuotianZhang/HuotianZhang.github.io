Where does the energy go in an organic solar cell?

I'm Huotian Zhang, a device physicist and postdoctoral researcher at the University of Potsdam. I combine ultrafast and steady-state spectroscopy with device modelling to trace how light becomes current in organic semiconductors, and where it is lost on the way. Before Potsdam I did my PhD and postdoc in Linköping, with stays at Imperial College London and FAU Erlangen.
